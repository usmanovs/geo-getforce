We ran this on ourselves first

Before offering this service, we audited and fixed getforce.org — our own AI education platform. Here's exactly what we found and shipped, with public tools verifying every claim.

Indexing bug

Found a structural bug blocking Bing from 8 of 9 subpages

A single-page app was silently serving the homepage's title and canonical tag on every route. Google's JS-rendering masked it; Bing's raw-HTML crawler saw every page as a duplicate of the homepage and refused to index them. Fixed by prerendering accurate HTML per route — verified live and confirmed in Bing Webmaster Tools' Live URL inspector.

Duplicate content

7 pages sharing one 201-character meta description

Seven separate pages — from the application form to the privacy policy — were all serving the exact same oversized description, which Bing flagged as a content error. Replaced with seven unique, correctly-sized descriptions, and re-verified via Bing's own Live URL test.

Automation

Automated indexing across three search engines

Set up a secure, read-only Google Search Console integration that resubmits the sitemap and reports indexing status automatically, an IndexNow integration for instant Bing/Yandex re-crawl pings, and verified ownership in Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and Yandex Webmaster.

GEO / structured data

Structured data built for AI engines, not just Google

Added Organization, Course, and genuine FAQPage schema — real questions pulled from our actual pricing and policies, not filler — so AI answer engines have accurate, citable facts instead of having to guess from prose.

Crawler access

Found our own robots.txt silently blocking AI crawlers

Added explicit allow rules for GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot — crawlers that are blocked by default on a lot of boilerplate robots.txt files without anyone noticing.

Local search

Set up and verified Google Business Profile

Configured as a service-area business with accurate category, description, and contact details, so local and "near me" search surfaces the business directly, alongside the organic site listing.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO gets your business found on Google and Bing search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your business cited and recommended by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. They overlap — clean structured data and crawlable pages help both — but GEO adds things SEO alone misses, like making sure AI crawlers aren't accidentally blocked and that your content is phrased in a way these engines can quote directly.

How do I know this actually works?

We ran this exact process on our own site, getforce.org, before offering it to anyone else. It's documented above: a structural bug that was silently blocking every subpage from Bing, duplicate meta descriptions across seven pages, missing structured data — all found and fixed, with before/after verification through Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and Yandex Webmaster.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes (indexing, structured data, crawler access) typically show up in Search Console and Webmaster Tools within days to a couple of weeks, since you're removing blockers rather than waiting on new rankings to build. Ranking and traffic growth from content and authority work takes longer — typically 2-3 months to show a clear trend.

Do you work with businesses outside Kyrgyzstan?

Yes. The technical work — structured data, search console automation, crawler accessibility — applies to any site regardless of location. Local search setup (like Google Business Profile) is most valuable for businesses with a physical or regional service area.

What if my site isn't built by you?

That's fine — most clients have existing sites on their own stack (WordPress, Shopify, custom code, Lovable, whatever). The audit starts by reading your actual codebase or site output, not a template checklist.

Do you guarantee specific rankings?

No reputable SEO provider can guarantee a specific ranking position — search engines don't sell that. What we do guarantee is removing technical blockers (crawl errors, indexing bugs, missing schema) and setting up the monitoring to prove it, with documented before/after evidence like we show above.
